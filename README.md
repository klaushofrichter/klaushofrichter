# Klaus Hofrichter

Software engineering executive based in the USA, with a background in cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and video platform development. Originally from Germany, Klaus holds a Master's Degree in Electronics Engineering from Technische Universität Berlin and relocated to the U.S. in 1999. He has held VP-level engineering roles at Eagle Eye Networks, Resideo, and Sony Electronics, and is affiliated with Skylar Technology.

His current open-source work focuses on Eagle Eye Networks integrations, OAuth tooling, and cloud-native applications built with Vue, TypeScript, and Kubernetes.
